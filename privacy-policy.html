2. Categories of Information We Collect

We collect information to provide high-quality yoga training, process inquiries, and ensure student safety. We categorize collected information as follows:

A. Information You Voluntarily Provide

  • Contact & Identity Details: When you submit a booking request for a free demo trial, consultation, or workshop, we collect your Full Name, Phone/WhatsApp Number, Email Address, Age, Gender, and City/Locality.
  • Training Preferences & Batch Timing: Your choice of yoga discipline (e.g., Morning General Fitness, Evening Weight Loss Vinyasa, Women’s Hormonal/PCOS Care, Kids Yoga, Spine/Cervical Rehabilitation, Competition Prep), preferred timing (Morning 5:00 AM – 8:30 AM or Evening 4:00 PM – 8:00 PM), and mode of instruction (In-Studio Bhilwara vs. Live Interactive Online).
  • Health & Medical Disclosures (Voluntary): In order to provide safe yoga postures and therapeutic adaptations, students may voluntarily share details regarding spinal stiffness, slip disc, joint arthritis, high blood pressure, recent surgeries, or pregnancy. This sensitive information is treated with strict medical confidentiality and accessible only to your certified yoga instructor.
  • Certificate Verification Inquiries: When using our verification portal (/certificates), queries regarding Student Roll Number, Certificate Serial, and Course Credentials.

B. Technical & Interaction Information Collected Automatically

  • Device & Browser Metadata: IP address, device type (Mobile, Tablet, Desktop), browser application (Chrome, Safari, Firefox), operating system, screen resolution, and language settings.
  • Log Data & Clickstream Analytics: Time and date of visit, pages viewed, time spent on individual service pages, referring URLs (such as Google Search, Facebook, or Instagram), and interaction with buttons or forms.

4. Advertising & Tracking Disclosures (Google Ads & Meta Business)

To connect individuals seeking authentic yoga training with our studio, Amrit Yoga Center participates in digital advertising networks, including Google Ads (Alphabet Inc.), Meta Business / Facebook & Instagram Ads (Meta Platforms, Inc.), and Google Business Profile.

A. Google Ads & Google Analytics 4 (GA4)

Our website uses Google Analytics 4 and Google Ads Conversion Tracking. These services employ cookies and tracking tags to gather aggregated, pseudonymous statistics:

  • Measurement & Conversion: We measure when a user visits our site from an ad and completes a meaningful interaction, such as clicking our phone link, sending a WhatsApp inquiry, or submitting a trial booking.
  • Remarketing & Audience Lists: We may display targeted, educational wellness advertisements on Google Search, YouTube, and the Google Display Network to prospective students who have previously expressed interest in our yoga classes.
  • Google Business Profile: Interacting with our verified Google Maps listing in Bhilwara may generate aggregated local search query reports (e.g., direction requests, click-to-call actions).

B. Meta Pixel (Facebook & Instagram Advertising)

We utilize the Meta Pixel provided by Meta Platforms, Inc. The Meta Pixel allows us to:

  • Measure Ad Effectiveness: Track visitor actions across devices after they view or click an advertisement on Facebook or Instagram.
  • Custom & Lookalike Audiences: Display relevant announcements regarding upcoming yoga batches, women's wellness workshops, and children's summer camps to users whose profile characteristics match health-conscious demographics.
  • Data Protection: Data collected via the Meta Pixel is encrypted via secure hashing before transmission, adhering to Meta’s Business Tools Terms.

C. EmailJS & Transactional Messaging Gateway

When you submit an admission or trial form, the communication is transmitted via EmailJS client-side encryption directly to our administration desk. EmailJS functions solely as a message relay and does not harvest or monetize your contact information.

5. Cookie Policy & How to Opt-Out

Cookies are small text files placed on your device to maintain site functionality, preserve user preferences (such as Dark/Light mode), and collect aggregated performance metrics.

  • Strictly Necessary Cookies: Essential for browsing, navigation, security checks, and form validation.
  • Performance & Analytics Cookies: Placed by Google Analytics to help us evaluate site traffic patterns and page loading speeds.
  • Marketing & Targeting Cookies: Placed by advertising partners (Google Ads, Meta Pixel) to deliver tailored promotions and prevent repetitive ad delivery.
How to Opt-Out of Personalized Advertising:
  • Google Ads Opt-Out: Customize or disable personalized Google Ads via Google Ad Settings.
  • Meta / Facebook Ad Settings: Manage what ads you see across Facebook & Instagram via Facebook Ad Preferences.
  • Industry-Wide Opt-Out Tools: Visit the Digital Advertising Alliance Consumer Choice Portal or Your Online Choices.
  • Browser Controls: You can configure your browser (Google Chrome, Apple Safari, Mozilla Firefox, Microsoft Edge) to reject all third-party cookies or alert you when a cookie is placed.

6. Information Sharing & Third-Party Disclosures

Zero Commercial Sale of Data: Amrit Yoga Center has never sold, rented, or monetized personal student information with commercial brokers or outside marketing aggregators, and will never do so.

We disclose information only under the following limited, legally regulated circumstances:

  • Authorized Technical Service Providers: Trusted cloud and database infrastructure vendors (such as Supabase PostgreSQL cloud, Vercel hosting, and EmailJS) who process data strictly under contractual confidentiality agreements.
  • Legal & Regulatory Mandates: Where required by valid court summons, governmental order, or applicable Indian law enforcement bodies to prevent fraud or protect physical safety.

7. Data Storage, Retention & Security Safeguards

We deploy robust organizational and technological safeguards to keep your personal data secure:

  • SSL/TLS Encryption: All communications between your web browser and our servers are encrypted using modern Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS).
  • Database Security: Dynamic certificate credentials and administration systems are hosted on Supabase PostgreSQL cloud infrastructure protected with Row Level Security (RLS) policies and parameterized queries to prevent SQL injection.
  • Data Retention Timelines:
    • Inquiry and trial class contact details are retained only for the duration necessary to deliver the requested service or maintain ongoing studio communications.
    • Student certificate records are maintained indefinitely on our public verification portal to provide lifelong verification proof for employment and competition qualification.

8. Student & User Rights (DPDP Act, 2023)

In accordance with Indian privacy laws and digital standards, you hold specific rights regarding your personal information:

  1. Right to Confirmation & Access: You may request a summary of the personal information we maintain concerning your enrollment or inquiries.
  2. Right to Correction & Rectification: If your phone number, email address, or name spelling is incorrect, you may request prompt corrections.
  3. Right to Erasure (Right to be Forgotten): You may request the deletion of your contact records from our inquiry databases, provided retention is not required by statutory regulations.
  4. Right to Withdraw Consent: You may opt out of promotional WhatsApp updates or marketing communications at any time by messaging "STOP" or notifying us via email.

To exercise any of these rights, write to our official email: contact@amrityogacenter.in.
